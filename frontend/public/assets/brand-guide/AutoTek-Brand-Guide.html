Primary mark

AutoTek

Velo — a diamond aperture (forward motion, open road) with a geometric AT monogram. Reads as both shield (trust) and arrowhead (speed). Wordmark is Inter 800, tracking −35.

Voice

Trustworthy, technical, fast. We don't over-promise — we show fitment. Plain words, no jargon. Celebrate local mechanics and the roads we serve.

Yes: "Brake pads for Toyota Hilux, delivered Monday."
No: "Revolutionary automotive aftermarket ecosystem."

Clear space

Padding = X

X = height of the diamond. Keep that zone clear of type, imagery, borders, or other marks on all sides.

Minimum size

16 px · 8 mm

Never render the mark smaller than 16 px on screen or 8 mm in print. Lockup minimum 112 px / 30 mm wide.

Typography

Inter · JetBrains Mono

Inter 400–800 for UI and marketing; JetBrains Mono 400–600 for technical specs, part numbers, receipts.

Usage

✓ Do — light
Teal on Gray 50 or white.
✓ Do — dark
Teal mark; white wordmark.
✗ Don't — distort
Never rotate, skew, or stretch.
✗ Don't — low contrast
Ensure 4.5:1 contrast.
Favicon & app icon

Identifiable at 16×16

The Velo diamond holds shape down to 16 px. Use the rounded Teal 700 container for iOS / Android app tiles; PWA maskable icons keep artwork within the inner 80% safe circle.

Background contrast

Pick mark by surface

Teal mark on light (Gray 50–100) and dark (Gray 900) surfaces. Use black on vivid Orange 500 accents. Never teal-on-teal.

AA minimum for mark-on-background contrast: 3:1 for logo, 4.5:1 for type.
